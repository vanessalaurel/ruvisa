# Ruvisa SEED Fund Pitch Deck — Minimal Canva Version

**Use this version for Canva / PowerPoint design.**  
Keep each slide visually clean: one big headline, 3-5 short points, one strong visual.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

### Ruvisa

**AI beauty decisions from selfie to skincare, makeup, and treatment.**

Ruvisa helps users understand their skin, choose the right products, track progress, and discover trusted beauty treatments.

**Design idea:**  
Use a clean hero layout: phone mockup on the right, short headline on the left. Add a soft beauty-tech gradient background, e.g. blush pink, cream, and deep rose.

---

## Slide 2 — Problem

### Beauty shopping is confusing, expensive, and hype-driven.

- Too many products, clinics, reviews, and promotions.
- Users do not know what fits their actual skin.
- Many purchases become trial-and-error waste.
- Hong Kong consumers spend heavily on skincare, yet nearly half report unused products.

**Design idea:**  
Show a messy shelf / product overload collage. Add small icons for “reviews,” “discounts,” “KOL hype,” “clinic offers,” and “budget.”

---

## Slide 3 — User Pain Point

### “I have a budget. What actually works for my skin?”

A user with acne scars, pigmentation, texture, or wrinkles must compare:

- skincare products
- routines
- makeup options
- clinic treatments
- discounts and first-trial offers

**Design idea:**  
Create a persona card: “22-year-old student, HKD 500 budget, acne scars.” Show arrows going to many confusing options, then Ruvisa as the single answer.

---

## Slide 4 — Market Opportunity

### Hong Kong first, Southeast Asia next.

- Hong Kong has high beauty spending and strong skincare culture.
- SEA beauty discovery is driven by Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, and KOLs.
- Consumers want personalization, transparency, and better value.
- Beauty clinics also need qualified leads.

**Design idea:**  
Use a map visual: Hong Kong highlighted, then arrows to Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, and the Philippines.

---

## Slide 5 — Solution

### Ruvisa is a free AI beauty journey.

Ruvisa helps users answer:

- What is happening to my skin?
- What product should I use next?
- What routine fits my budget?
- Is my skin improving?
- Do I need product, makeup, or treatment?

**Design idea:**  
Use a circular journey diagram: Free Scan → AI Analysis → Product/Routine → Track Progress → Treatment Booking → Smarter Recommendation.

---

## Slide 6 — Product Flow

### From selfie to recommendation.

1. Register or login
2. Upload selfie
3. Get skin analysis
4. Receive product and routine recommendations
5. Ask Ruvisa AI why
6. Track progress over time

**Design idea:**  
Use 6 mobile screens or simple UI cards in a left-to-right flow. Keep each step as one icon + one phrase.

---

## Slide 7 — Technology

### Our AI stack makes beauty recommendations explainable.

- **Skin Analyzer:** detects concerns from selfie.
- **Product Intelligence:** reads ingredients, claims, and reviews.
- **Adaptive Recommender:** matches skin needs to products and routines.
- **Agentic AI:** explains recommendations and calls trusted tools.

**Design idea:**  
Use a 4-layer stack diagram. Give each layer a different color. Add “shared skin concern vector” as the connector across all layers.

# Canva Style Guide

## Visual Theme

- **Style:** premium beauty-tech, clean, modern, trustworthy
- **Colors:** blush pink, cream, white, deep rose, muted teal
- **Fonts:** elegant display font for titles, simple sans-serif for body
- **Mood:** scientific but warm, not medical or too corporate

## Design Rules

- Use one main idea per slide.
- Keep text under 40-60 words per slide when possible.
- Use icons, app screenshots, cards, and diagrams instead of paragraphs.
- Put detailed explanations in speaker notes, not on the slide.
- Use consistent footer: `Ruvisa | SEED Fund Pitch`.
